Google Material Design

Google published Material Design in 2014 and has revised it continuously since, currently as Material 3. It is a design language written down as a public specification, complete with measurements, motion curves, and worked examples, so that thousands of apps built by different companies on different continents can follow one rulebook and still feel like they belong to the same world. The components below are recreations of what the spec pages show.
