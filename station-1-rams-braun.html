Dieter Rams / Braun

Dieter Rams ran design at Braun from 1961 to 1995, and across those decades he gave hundreds of unrelated household products, from radios and record players to clocks, calculators, and shelving, a single shared visual logic: neutral surfaces, controls arranged by function, and color reserved for the one element the user needs to find first. The objects below are classroom recreations of his ideas rather than the originals, but the logic they follow is his.

Ten Principles for Good Design

Dieter Rams. Condensed; canonical text at Vitsœ.
  1. Good design is innovative
  2. Good design makes a product useful
  3. Good design is aesthetic
  4. Good design makes a product understandable
  5. Good design is unobtrusive
  6. Good design is honest
  7. Good design is long-lasting
  8. Good design is thorough, down to the last detail
  9. Good design is environmentally friendly
  10. Good design is as little design as possible
