Sophomore. I like building things, mostly small tools that solve one problem at a time. This page collects three projects from this year, what each one does, and what I learned making it.

Projects

Practice Room Scheduler. A sign-up tool for the music wing's four practice rooms. Before this, sign-ups were a paper sheet that disappeared every other week. The tool takes a name and a time slot, blocks double-booking, and posts the day's schedule to a shared screen. Built with Replit and a small database. What I learned: the hard part was not the code, it was deciding what happens when two people want 3:00.

Snow Day Predictor. A page that pulls tomorrow's forecast and computes a snow-day probability from temperature, precipitation, and timing, using six years of past closures I collected from old announcements. It has been right twice and wrong once. What I learned: a model is only as good as the data you feed it, and six years is not much data.

Lunch Line Counter. A tally app the student council used for one week to time the lunch lines. It records a timestamp per person and charts the wait by minute. The data went into a proposal to open the second line ten minutes earlier. What I learned: nobody argues with a chart of their own cafeteria.
